Wegovy injection dose schedule

US label revised 6/2026 · Checked 31 October 2026

Quick answer

Wegovy (semaglutide) is injected once a week. The label starts at 0.25 mg for 4 weeks, then steps up every 4 weeks: 0.5 mg, 1 mg, 1.7 mg, and 2.4 mg from week 17. The label’s ongoing dose is 2.4 mg, or 1.7 mg.

From week 17 the label’s ongoing dose is 2.4 mg, or 1.7 mg. For weight reduction in adults who have taken 2.4 mg for at least 4 weeks, the label allows going up to 7.2 mg if more weight reduction is needed.

Good to know

  • Inject once a week on the same day, at any time of day, with or without meals.
  • If a step isn’t tolerated, the label suggests considering waiting 4 more weeks before the next one.

If a dose is missed

If the next dose is more than 2 days away, take it as soon as possible. If it's less than 2 days away, skip it and take the next one on the usual day. After 2 or more missed doses in a row, the label says to restart the steps at a lower dose.

Changing the dose day

Inject on the same day each week.

Questions about Wegovy injection dosing

What is the starting dose of Wegovy?

The label starts Wegovy at 0.25 mg once a week for the first 4 weeks. The dose then steps up every 4 weeks, to 0.5 mg, 1 mg and 1.7 mg, reaching 2.4 mg at week 17.

How long does it take to reach 2.4 mg?

On the label’s schedule, 16 weeks: 2.4 mg starts at week 17. A prescriber may wait longer between steps if a dose isn’t tolerated.

What is Wegovy 7.2 mg?

Wegovy HD. The label allows 7.2 mg once a week for weight reduction in adults who have taken 2.4 mg for at least 4 weeks and need more weight reduction. It is the highest dose on the label.

What if I miss a Wegovy dose?

The label says to take it as soon as possible if the next dose is more than 2 days away, and otherwise to skip it. After 2 or more missed doses in a row, it says to restart at a lower dose.

Can I switch between Wegovy injections and tablets?

The label describes how: 25 mg tablets start one week after a last 2.4 mg injection, and 2.4 mg injections start the day after a last 25 mg tablet. Your prescriber sets the plan.
