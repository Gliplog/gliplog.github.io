Rybelsus dose schedule

From the US prescribing information, revised 1/2026 · Last checked against the label 31 October 2026

Quick answer

Rybelsus (semaglutide) is taken once a day. The label starts at 3 mg for 30 days, then 7 mg. From day 61, if more blood sugar control is needed, it can go up to 14 mg.

Rybelsus titration schedule

Approved for type 2 diabetes, and other uses on its label. Your prescriber decides your dose and when to change it.

Days on each dose

  1. 3 mg Days 1 to 30
  2. 7 mg Day 31 on
  3. 14 mg If needed, from day 61

The label’s ongoing dose is 7 mg once a day, or 14 mg if more blood sugar control is needed.

Good to know

  • One tablet each morning on an empty stomach, swallowed whole with a little plain water (up to 4 ounces, about 120 mL). Wait at least 30 minutes before eating, drinking, or taking other medicines by mouth. Never more than one tablet a day.
  • Rybelsus and Ozempic tablets are not swapped milligram for milligram: the label pairs Rybelsus 7 mg with Ozempic 4 mg tablets, and 14 mg with 9 mg. It says not to switch between them in the first 30 days.

If a dose is missed

Skip the missed tablet and take the next one the following day.

Questions about Rybelsus dosing

What is the starting dose of Rybelsus?

The label starts Rybelsus at 3 mg once a day for 30 days. It notes that 3 mg doesn’t lower blood sugar: it is there to start. On day 31 the dose goes up to 7 mg.

When can Rybelsus go up to 14 mg?

From day 61, if more blood sugar control is needed, according to the label. Your prescriber decides.

Is Rybelsus the same as Ozempic tablets?

Both are semaglutide tablets on one label, but in different strengths that the label says are not swapped milligram for milligram.

What if I miss a Rybelsus tablet?

The label says to skip it and take the next tablet the following day.
