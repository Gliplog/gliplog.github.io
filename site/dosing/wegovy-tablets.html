Wegovy tablets dose schedule

US label revised 6/2026 · Checked 31 October 2026

Quick answer

Wegovy tablets (semaglutide) are taken once a day. The label steps up every 30 days: 1.5 mg for days 1 to 30, 4 mg for days 31 to 60, 9 mg for days 61 to 90, then the ongoing dose of 25 mg from day 91.

The label’s ongoing dose is 25 mg once a day. If 25 mg isn’t tolerated, the label suggests considering the weekly Wegovy injection at 1.7 mg instead.

Good to know

  • One tablet each morning on an empty stomach, swallowed whole with up to 4 ounces (120 mL) of plain water. Wait 30 minutes before eating, drinking, or taking other pills. One tablet a day at most.
  • If a step isn’t tolerated, the label suggests considering a delay before the next one.
  • Switching: the label starts 25 mg tablets one week after a last 2.4 mg injection, and 2.4 mg injections the day after a last 25 mg tablet.

If a dose is missed

Skip the missed tablet and take the next one the following day.

Questions about Wegovy tablets dosing

What is the starting dose of Wegovy tablets?

The label starts Wegovy tablets at 1.5 mg once a day for the first 30 days, then steps up every 30 days to 4 mg and 9 mg, reaching 25 mg on day 91.

How should Wegovy tablets be taken?

The label says one tablet each morning on an empty stomach with up to 4 ounces of plain water, swallowed whole, then wait at least 30 minutes before food, other drinks or other medicines by mouth.

What if I miss a Wegovy tablet?

The label says to skip it and take the next tablet the following day.

What if 25 mg isn’t tolerated?

The label suggests considering switching to the weekly Wegovy injection at 1.7 mg. Your prescriber decides.
