Privacy policy

Tidebook collects no personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What Tidebook stores

When you use Tidebook it stores what you enter: your medications, doses, weigh-ins, injection sites, notes, and any profile details you choose to add (height, birth month and year, gender, goal weight), plus your display settings. Every profile field is optional.

This data is kept only on your device: in your browser's storage when you use the web app, or inside the app's private storage on Android.

What Tidebook sends

Nothing you enter. Tidebook has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code. The web app loads its own files from GitHub Pages, then works offline; when you are online, your browser checks GitHub Pages now and then for a newer version of the app, and that request carries none of your data. The Android app requests no network permission and makes no requests at all.

GitHub, which hosts these pages, may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) when your browser downloads them, under GitHub's privacy statement. Those logs never include anything you enter into Tidebook.

Backups

You can save a backup file from Settings. It is a plain JSON file containing your data, and it goes wherever you choose to put it. Tidebook never uploads it. Android cloud backup is turned off for the app, so your data is not copied to Google's servers.

Deleting your data

Use Settings, Erase everything, to delete all of it from the device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing this site's data in your browser, also deletes it. Because there is no copy anywhere else, deleted data cannot be recovered unless you kept a backup.

Children

Tidebook is intended for adults and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. The source code, including this page, is public at github.com/Tidebook.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be raised as an issue at github.com/Tidebook/app/issues.
