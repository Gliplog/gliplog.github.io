Privacy policy

Tidebook collects no personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What Tidebook stores

Your medicines, doses, weigh-ins, injection sites, notes, display settings and any profile details you add (height, birth month and year, gender, goal weight). They are stored only on your device: in your browser's storage, or in the Android app's private storage.

What Tidebook sends

Nothing you enter. There is no account, server, analytics, advertising or third-party code. The web app loads its files from GitHub Pages and then works offline. When you're online, your browser occasionally checks for a newer version, and that check carries none of your data. The Android app has no network permission.

GitHub hosts these pages and may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under GitHub's privacy statement. Those logs never include anything you enter in Tidebook.

Backups

A backup from Settings is a plain JSON file that goes wherever you save it. Tidebook never uploads it, and Android cloud backup is turned off for the app.

Deleting your data

Settings, Erase everything deletes all your data from the device. So does uninstalling the app or clearing this site's data in your browser. Without a backup, deleted data can't be recovered.

Children

Tidebook is intended for adults and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will appear on this page with a new date. Tidebook's source code, including this page, is public at github.com/Tidebook under the GNU AGPL-3.0. For privacy questions, open an issue at github.com/Tidebook/app/issues.
